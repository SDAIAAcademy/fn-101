The check wants a frozen data class without a phone or identity field, opened as a date, the right age and the exact order of all 246 tickets. Common errors: sorting by t.priority, which is alphabetical; forgetting the status filter (400 tickets); reverse=True to get urgent first, which also puts the newest first; keeping opened as text, which sorts correctly by luck in this format and fails age_days.
