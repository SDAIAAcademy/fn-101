# Notebook, script or module?

Four tasks from the analysts of Manahil. For each, write one word (notebook, script or module)
after the number, then "because" and one line of reason.

Task 1. Badr asks, once: "Did the tickets about low pressure in Al-Rabwa go up after the
pipe work in week two?" Nobody knows yet which columns matter or what the answer looks like.

Task 2. On the first working day of every month, whoever is on duty must turn the new
readings file into the monthly report, the same way each time, with one command.

Task 3. The rule "is this reading an anomaly?" is needed by the monthly report, by the ticket
tool that Badr's team uses, and by the tests of both.

Task 4. Salma wants to try three thresholds, look at the three lists side by side with a
short note on each, and show the page to her manager before one threshold is chosen.

1: notebook because the question is asked once and the way to the answer is not known yet: looking at each step is the work.
2: script because it is the same steps every month, run by different people, and must not depend on what anyone ran before.
3: module because three other pieces of code need the same rule: it is written once, imported, and tested in one place.
4: notebook because the result is a page to read and discuss, with outputs and notes together, and the choice is still open.
